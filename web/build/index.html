Prevailing West Bengal Stamp Duty and Registration Rates

Under the West Bengal Directorate of Registration and Stamp Revenue, property stamp duty is assessed at 6% for properties valued above ₹40 Lakhs in municipal corporation areas (5% in non-corporation and panchayat jurisdictions), plus a mandatory 1% registration fee. Stamp duty is calculated based on the higher of the transaction consideration or the official circle rate assessed on the e-Nathikaran system.

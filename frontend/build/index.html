Prevailing West Bengal Stamp Duty and Registration Rates.

Property stamp duty in West Bengal is assessed under the Directorate of Registration and Stamp Revenue (e-Nathikaran). For residential properties valued above ₹40 Lakhs in municipal corporation areas like KMC, standard stamp duty is 6%. In non-corporation and gram panchayat jurisdictions across West Bengal, the applicable statutory stamp duty rate is 5%. A mandatory 1% statutory registration fee applies to all residential property conveyances throughout the state. Stamp duty is calculated on whichever is higher between the registered transaction value and the official government circle rate.
